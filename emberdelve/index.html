Emberdelve.

A turn-based dice roguelite built on one rule: every death has to be fair. Enemy intent is always visible before you commit, dice resolve by rules you can learn, and runs are seeded — the same choices always play out the same way.

What's inside

Readable dice combat where every enemy telegraphs its intent · seeded runs with a daily delve everyone plays on the same seed · the Delver's Ledger, a growing book of achievements that recognises how you play (no daily chores, nothing expires) · new enemies, bosses and events landing in near-weekly updates. Built solo with Flutter + Flame under the Tsoro Studios banner in Kwekwe, Zimbabwe. Found a bug, or think something's unfair? Tell me — blunt feedback shapes the game.
