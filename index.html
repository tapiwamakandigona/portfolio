About

I'm a self-taught developer from Kwekwe, Zimbabwe. I started building out of curiosity — first small scripts, then full products. Today that ranges from production web apps like VoltZW, a ZESA prepaid electricity calculator, to Android platform work — custom GKI kernel builds and system apps for my own phone — to games and data projects like the Zimbabwe Language Data Commons.

My stack is TypeScript + React/Next.js with Appwrite or Supabase on the backend, Kotlin and Flutter for Android, and Three.js when I need 3D. I like building in the open — most of my work is on GitHub.

Outside of code: AI olympiads (bronze medalist at the African Olympiad in AI), game design, and trying to make things that people in Zimbabwe actually use.
